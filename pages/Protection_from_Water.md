Protects the object or creature from water and keeps it from getting wet. If it is cast on a creature it also protects any objects it is wearing or carrying.

## Casting

To cast Protection from Water:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Protection, Water vs DR 7
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Protection, Water Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
