Death by Frost is the most powerful Ice spell. It is a single target spell which does d+5 + 1 per level in damage for 3 rounds. It also reduces status by 1 per level. Armor can not block this effect due to the fact that the target gets frozen from the inside.

## Casting

To cast Death by Frost:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Ice vs DR 17
- **Cost**: 6 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 5 round
- **Cooldown**: 6 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Ice Attunement 5 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 months ritual to transfer spell ownership
