Gives a strong push from the direction of the caster. If the level equals the size of the target, he must make a coordination roll of 5 (+2 per level over the size) or fall. Damage from falling is as from falling 1 m. It can push 5 kg 1 m backwards on stone surface per level.

## Casting

To cast Push:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air vs DR 7
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 6 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
