Calls a storm out of a clear sky, or sends an honest storm elsewhere. The weather arrives within the hour and is genuinely dangerous to shipping, roofs and tempers. Walmaer and Shalissa priests frown on amateurs using this.

### Ingredients

- Seawater taken in foul weather (1 l).
- A bell rung once and then silenced.\*
- Petrel feathers (3).
- Powdered flint (20 g).
- A shouted argument with the sky.
