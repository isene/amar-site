Enables the caster to read the thoughts of the target. At level 1 you can read words, at level 3 pictures and at level 5 concepts. You can only read what the target is thinking about, you can not search through his mind for information.

## Casting

To cast Read Thought:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Life, Perception vs DR 13
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Life, Perception Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
