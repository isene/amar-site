Allows you to give the undead a command consisting of up to 5 words per level. If the undead is intelligent it must understand the command or the spell does not work. If the undead is intelligent the magic defense is doubled if a command goes against the moral code of the target, and tripled if it is self destructive.

## Casting

To cast Command Undead:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Black vs DR 13
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Black Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
