The drinker's belly fills with living fire: once per round for three rounds they may breathe a cone of flame 5 m long (damage 5, DEF avoids half). Speaking while it lasts is unwise, and hiccups are dangerous for everyone.

### Ingredients

- Peppers dried over a smith's forge (20 g).
- Salamander ash (5 g).
- Strong spirits (1 dl).
- Oil that has burnt in a temple lamp (1 dl).\*
- Swallowed in one go.
