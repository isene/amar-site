Creates a soft puff of wind in the direction the caster decides. Higher levels creates a stronger puff, but even a level 10 spell is not going to bowl someone over.

## Casting

To cast Puff of Wind:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air vs DR 6
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
