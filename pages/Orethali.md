Orethali is a village of some 200 people (about 40 houses) where the forest road meets the eastern sea.

The rocky shore faces east; dense forest covers everything west. A small jetty and drying racks serve the fishing boats.

Orethali's fishers know the eastern currents better than anyone, and know which waters to avoid.
