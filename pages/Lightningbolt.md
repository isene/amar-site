The lightningbolt delivers an electric shock which does d-1 + 1 per level in damage. Metal armor does not protect against this.

In water the electric shock spreads out in all directions.

The skill of [Pointing](Combat.html#Missile_Weapons) is used to determine if this spell hits its intended target.

## Casting

To cast Lightningbolt:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air, Water vs DR 11
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 8 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air, Water Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
