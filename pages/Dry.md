Dries out the object(s) and/or creature(s) targeted. Note that when used on living creatures (including plants) it only dries the surface, not the body itself. The fire version leaves the target(s) slightly warm.

## Casting

To cast Dry:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air, Earth, Fire, Water vs DR 7
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 min.
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air, Earth, Fire, Water Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
