### General description

The pigs on Amar resemble the medieval European pig, and look like a cross between a modern pig and a wild boar. They are also wilder than modern pigs. Male’s chainteeth are sometimes visible. They are usually kept in forests.

### Diet

Pigs are omnivorous. They eat any­thing from garbage to fruits and hens.

### Mental description

The domesticated pigs are more mild mannered and less eager to attack. Old males may be extremely grouchy though. Many stories tell of children who have teased old males and had to pay for it with their lives.
