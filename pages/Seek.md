Gives the missile +2 per level to hit for the next shot. The missile is considered magick. Each consecutive seek spell on a missile gives it an extra shot.

## Casting

To cast Seek:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air vs DR 11
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
