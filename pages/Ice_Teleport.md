Put the object or creature in a bubble and teleports it. It is only possible to teleport in ice.

## Casting

To cast Ice Teleport:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Ice vs DR 15
- **Cost**: 5 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Ice Attunement 4 or higher
- **Giving**: 4 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
