Freezes an object for the duration of the spell. If something or someone comes in contact with the frozen object, the ice will spread over and freeze that object or bodypart too. After the duration of the spell the ice will disappear and the temperature will go up.

## Casting

To cast Frozen Trap:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Ice vs DR 11
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 2 rounds
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Ice Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
