This ritual will make the victim blind for one month. It actually affects the mind of the victim so there are no visible physical effects.

### Ingredients

- A part of the target’s body (a hair, nail or the like). A larger part like a finger will decrease the difficulty rating of the spell by up to 3.
- A black lotus burned.
- A 30 carat onyx.\*
