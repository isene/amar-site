Gives 1 AP per level to the creature or object it is cast on. If cast on a creature, it does not protect any equipment it may carry.

## Casting

To cast Armor:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Earth, Protection vs DR 11
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Earth, Protection Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
