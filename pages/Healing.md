Heals 1 Body Point per level at the rate of 1 BP per 30 minutes. The spell lasts until it has healed its maximum number of BP or until the target is completely healed, whichever comes first. Healing a broken bone requires a level 4 healing. To reattach a severed body part you must apply a level 7 healing within 2 hours.

## Casting

To cast Healing:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Life vs DR 9
- **Cost**: 2 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 min.
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Life Attunement 2 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
