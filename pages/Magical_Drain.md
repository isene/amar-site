Draines 1 Magical Aptitude from the target. The range gets increased each spell level. The spell uses 1 BP of the caster. The target will regain the magical aptitude after a good night sleep.

Can not reduce the target's MA below 0.

## Casting

To cast Magical Drain:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Black vs DR 7
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 3 rounds
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Black Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
