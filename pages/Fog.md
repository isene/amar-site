Creates fog. Sight within the fog is limited to 2 meters, and combat modifiers for moonlight applies.

## Casting

To cast Fog:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air, Water vs DR 9
- **Cost**: 2 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 min.
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air, Water Attunement 2 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
