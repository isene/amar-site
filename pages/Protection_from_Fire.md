Gives 3 armor points per level against fire damage. It also protects any objects carried by the target.

## Casting

To cast Protection from Fire:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Earth, Fire, Protection, Water vs DR 11
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Earth, Fire, Protection, Water Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
