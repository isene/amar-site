Reduses the effect of wounds by 1 BP +1 per full 5 levels for the purpose of half or quarter action, uncounsiousness and disabling. It will never make the target more resistant to damage than a zombie.

## Casting

To cast Ease Pain:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Life vs DR 7
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 8 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Life Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
