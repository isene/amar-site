### Divine Characteristics

- **BODY**: 5 (Divine tier - transcends mortal limits)
- **MIND**: 5 (Divine tier - infinite knowledge)
- **SPIRIT**: 10 (Supreme - inventor of spell magick)

### Divine Powers

Fenimaal has mastery of all SPIRIT → Casting skills:

- **Range**: Unlimited
- **Duration**: Unlimited
- **Area of Effect**: Unlimited
- **Weight**: Unlimited
- **Number of Targets**: Unlimited

All SPIRIT → Attunement skills at maximum (10+):

- Complete mastery of all magical domains

### Description

Fenimaal is the God that invented the Magick Spells. His worshippers build upon his invention and create new spells. His worshippers are wizards, especially those who research new spells and especially those who practice the Magick path.

### Worship in the 3-Tier System

- **SPIRIT → Worship → Fenimaal** skill represents devotion and connection
- Priests gain spell-creation abilities as they advance
- Mages (priest rank) can research and create new spells
- Divine boons may include temporary boosts to Casting attributes or new spell gifts
