Asteria is the Queen of the North, the Mother of Cold, the Ice Lady. She is cool, calm and collected and said to actually have a heart of ice. Asteria is the ultimate Frost Mage and commands all things cold.

Her followers aspire to be as cool and as commanding of the snow as they can be. She has many dwarven worshippers.
