Sends a summoned demon back to the place it was summoned from.

## Casting

To cast Banish:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air, Black, Earth, Fire, Life, Summoning, Water vs DR 13
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air, Black, Earth, Fire, Life, Summoning, Water Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
