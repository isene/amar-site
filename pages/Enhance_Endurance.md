Increases the endurance of the recipient by 20 % per level.

## Casting

To cast Enhance Endurance:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Life vs DR 9
- **Cost**: 2 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 10 min.
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Life Attunement 2 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
