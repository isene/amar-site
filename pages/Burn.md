Inside the area of effect every creature will burn and take D-2 + 1 per level in damage. Metal armor does not protect against this.

## Casting

To cast Burn:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Heat vs DR 11
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 2 rounds
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Heat Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
