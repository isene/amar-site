Turns the target into any creature of any sex. The caster must have possessed a creature of the same race and sex. The size of the target may not change more than +20 % or -10 % per level (-1 % per level above level 9). The strength, endurance, coordination and awareness of the target will usually be modified at the GM’s discretion based on the transformation.

## Casting

To cast Polymorph:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Life, Water vs DR 15
- **Cost**: 5 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 min.
- **Cooldown**: 8 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Life, Water Attunement 5 or higher
- **Giving**: 3 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
