---
title: Half Elf
---

Half Elves are one of the [Playable Races](Playable_Races.html) in Amar RPG.

### Physical description

Half Elves look like humans except for being a bit smaller and more slender.

### Special skills

Half Elves generally has higher Awareness then humans (average of 5 with a potential of 18), night vision and has skills that help with the forest life. They have a potential in Strength of 12 (male) and 10 (females). Their expected lifespan is 150.
