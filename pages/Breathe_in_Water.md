Enables the target to breathe in water as if it was air. It only works on air breathing creatures.

## Casting

To cast Breathe in Water:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air, Life, Water vs DR 11
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air, Life, Water Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
