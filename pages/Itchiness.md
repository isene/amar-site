Give the target an itch in a body part. The affected body part will get a -1 status + -1 per two levels, so every skill done by this body part will be harder to execute. The spell cannot be used on the targets head and will not give a -1 status to every skill.

## Casting

To cast Itchiness:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Black vs DR 7
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 7 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Black Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
