The drinker's skin greys and hardens to living stone: +2 Armor Points on all hit locations, and unarmed blows against the drinker hurt the striker. While it lasts, swimming is a very bad idea.

### Ingredients

- Granite dust from a standing stone (50 g).
- A gargoyle's chip or a petrified twig (10 g).
- Egg of a cliff-nesting bird.
- Iron filings (10 g).
- Buried three days in the earth.
