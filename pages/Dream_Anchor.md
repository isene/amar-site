Drunk at bedtime with a person or place firmly in mind, the potion steers the night's dream there: true glimpses mixed with dream-stuff, useful to those wise enough to tell which is which. Ielina initiates prize it.

### Ingredients

- Poppy seeds (5 g).
- A silver sliver of a mirror.\*
- Lavender (10 g).
- Water that has reflected the moon (1 dl).
- Something belonging to the person or place, held while falling asleep.
