By performing this ritual, the area arnound the character will become dark and the [Raven Demon](https://d6gaming.org/index.php/Raven_Demon) will summon. When the demon is encountered the player must do a fear roll of D6+3. If the character has done this ritual before, the ingredients are not needed and the DR is decreased by 3.

### Ingredients

- A Raven Feather.
- An Eye From A Wolf.
- Ash From A Torch
