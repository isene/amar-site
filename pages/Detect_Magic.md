Increases the MA by 3 per level for the purpose of detecting magic only.

## Casting

To cast Detect Magic:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Magic, Perception vs DR 6
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Magic, Perception Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
