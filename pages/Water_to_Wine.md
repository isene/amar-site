Turns water into the wine of your choice. The quality of the wine increases with the levels.

## Casting

To cast Water to Wine:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Water vs DR 7
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 2 rounds
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Water Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
