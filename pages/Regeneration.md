Regenerates any one limb, finger, nose, etc. which has been cut off. An arm regenerates in one week, a leg in two weeks. The spell lasts until the missing body part has regenerated.

## Casting

To cast Regeneration:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Life vs DR 15
- **Cost**: 5 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 hour
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Life Attunement 5 or higher
- **Giving**: 3 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
