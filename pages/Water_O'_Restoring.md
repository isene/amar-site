Creates fresh water capable of healing creatures who drink it. The water heals a creature 2BP, over 30 minutes, per liter that is drunk.

## Casting

To cast Water O' Restoring:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Water vs DR 10
- **Cost**: 2 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 min.
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Water Attunement 2 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
