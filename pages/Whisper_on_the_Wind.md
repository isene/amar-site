The caster speaks a message of at most twenty words into a feather. The wind carries it, and within the hour the named person hears it whispered as if someone stood behind them. The wind does not carry an answer back.

### Ingredients

- A feather from a bird that has seen the recipient.
- Breath, exhaled through a silver ring.\*
- Dried thyme, burnt (5 g).
- The recipient's name, spoken thrice.
