The spoken table will give you an idea of what the different skill values represent in terms of ability to speak a language.

| Skill | Proficiency                                                |
|-------|------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1     | Simple words and phrases                                   |
| 2     | Understand most common speech, difficulty in finding words |
| 3–7   | Normal speech                                              |
| 8     | Good vocabulary                                            |
| 9     | Many special words and phrases of many social groups       |
| 10+   | Extreme command of language                                |
