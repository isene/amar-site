The skill of dodging incoming blows in a combat (an Athletics skill). It gives +1 in defensive value per full 5 in Dodge skill total. When purely dodging (forgoing all attacks), your defensive value is your Dodge total - 2. This is explained further in the chapter on [Combat](Combat.html).

This skill is also used for such things as dodging a falling rock, avoid falling into a pit that opens beneath you, etc.
