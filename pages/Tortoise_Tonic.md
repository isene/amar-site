Slow and certain: the drinker's Move is halved, but they gain +2 Defensive value and cannot be knocked down or staggered. Popular with shield-wall veterans and nervous travellers on mountain paths.

### Ingredients

- A tortoise scute, powdered (5 g).
- Oak bark (20 g).
- Thick porridge water (2 dl).
- Patience, stirred in slowly.
