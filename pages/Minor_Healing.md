Heals 1 BP + 1 per full 2 levels during the week it lasts. It only heals damage received before the spell is cast. Healing a broken bone requires a level 6 healing.

## Casting

To cast Minor Healing:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Life vs DR 6
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Life Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
