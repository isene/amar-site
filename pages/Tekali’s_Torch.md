This ritual must be performed on a crystal to take effect. It will illuminate an area as a torch, except that the light created is homogeneous.

### Ingredients

- A 5 carat diamond.\*
- Ash from a torch.
- A sunflower.
- Crushed whale bones (100 g).
- An endurance roll of 8.
