Molthaki is a village of some 250 people (about 50 houses) in a forest clearing west of Keron, on the same road.

Fields and grazing fill the clearing, with the forest all around. A smaller track leads northeast toward Caer Ilias.

The Baron's riders stable fresh horses here on the road between the castle and the coast.
