Lets the caster know if the target believes he is telling the truth or not. The spell is broken if the target moves out of range or out of sight.

## Casting

To cast Detect Truth:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Perception vs DR 9
- **Cost**: 2 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Perception Attunement 2 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
