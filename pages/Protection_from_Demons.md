A demon must exceed the target’s MD +2 per level (+4 per level if the spell is active) with its MD+d in order to attack him.

## Casting

To cast Protection from Demons:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Black, Life, Protection, Summoning vs DR 11
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Black, Life, Protection, Summoning Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
