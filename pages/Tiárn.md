Tiárn is a stone tower keep at the western end of the mountain lake northwest of Amaron, guarding the northern pass. A garrison of a few dozen holds it for the King.

The mountains rise steep to the north and west; the lake stretches east. A trail follows the lakeshore south toward the capital, and a small jetty serves the keep's boat.

Nothing crosses the pass or the lake unseen by Tiárn's watch.
