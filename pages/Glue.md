Glues the touching surface of two objects together. The spell holds 50 kg per level, and requires a strength of 2 per level to break apart. Caution: If the glue is strong, you may tear the surface off the object instead of breaking the glue.

## Casting

To cast Glue:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Earth vs DR 9
- **Cost**: 2 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Earth Attunement 2 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
