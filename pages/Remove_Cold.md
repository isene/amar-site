## Casting

To cast Remove Cold:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Life vs DR 7
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 10 min.
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Life Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
