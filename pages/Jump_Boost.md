Boosts a creatures jump height. Each level the creature's speed will be increased by 25%.

## Casting

To cast Jump Boost:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air vs DR 7
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
