Brewed over a hair of another person, the potion reshapes the drinker's face and voice to theirs for an hour. Height, build and memories stay the drinker's own. Wears off all at once, usually at the worst possible moment.

### Ingredients

- A hair of the person to be worn.
- Soft wax (30 g).
- Milk of a black goat (1 dl).
- A pinch of stage rouge.
- Brewed before a covered mirror.
