Cleans the object(s) thoroughly leaving them gleaming as if polished. The weight is the total weight of all the objects affected. Whether such things as paint, etc. is removed depends on the intention of the caster. The air version blows the dirt away, the earth version leaves the dirt on the ground, the fire version leaves the objects varm (a result of 6 ignites flammable objects), the water version leaves the objects damp.

## Casting

To cast Polish:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air, Earth, Fire, Water vs DR 6
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 5 rounds
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air, Earth, Fire, Water Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
