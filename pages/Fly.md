Enables the target to fly at a speed of 2 m/s per level and dive at a speed of 4 m/s per level.

## Casting

To cast Fly:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air vs DR 15
- **Cost**: 5 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air Attunement 4 or higher
- **Giving**: 3 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
