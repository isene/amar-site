This potion will cause the person to fight with more aggression and strength. It will also increase the endurance so as to increase the body points. The endurance will be increased by 3 and the strength will be doubled. Since the person drinking the potion will fight like a berserker, he subtracts five from the defensive value and adds three to the offensive value. He may not cast any spells during combat. The person will be very irritable while under the influence of this potion.

### Ingredients

- Vervain (10 g).
- Fly amanita (2 g).
- Troll blood (1/4 dl).
- Blood from a cave lion killed in single combat (1/2 dl).
