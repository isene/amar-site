Changes the size of the target by up to +20 % or -10 % of the weight per level. Above level 9 the size decreases by 1 % per level.

## Casting

To cast Change Size:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Life, Water vs DR 11
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 min.
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Life, Water Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
