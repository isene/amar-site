---
title: Lizard Man (Playable Race)
---

Lizard Men are one of the [Playable Races](Playable_Races.html) in Amar RPG.

### Physical description

Lizard men are large, intelligent, humanoid reptiles. They have a long tail used to coordinate and speed up their swimming. Their skin is made up of small scales making the lizard men well protected. They are partially cold blooded, and if the temperature drops below 15°C they get slower and lose coordination. At 0°C they can barely move.

### Special Skills

Lizard men are fantastic swimmers and can hold their breath for 30 minutes. Lizard men has a higher swimming skill then humanoids and have night vision. Lizard men has a lower riding skill then other humanoids and has a -3 in riding potential. Their skin give them armor points from 1 and higher.
