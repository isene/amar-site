Words come easy and land well: +3 on Bargain and Social Lore. The drinker also finds it unusually hard to stop talking.

### Ingredients

- Honey mead (1 dl).
- A silver coin, dissolved slowly.\*
- Petals of sweet violet (10 g).
- A compliment, spoken into the bottle and corked quickly.
