The drinker's heart grows light: immune to fear (no rolls on the fear table) and impossible to intimidate. Also very hard to take anything seriously, which has ended more than one important negotiation.

### Ingredients

- Wine from a wedding (2 dl).
- Honey (50 g).
- A jester's bell, dipped and removed.\*
- Feverfew (10 g).
- A genuinely funny story, told to the brew.
