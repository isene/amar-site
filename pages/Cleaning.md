Cleans the area, comparable to sweeping, dusting, mopping. Does not get rid of really ingrained dirt. Any potensial dirt that is created during the duration will be cleaned.

## Casting

To cast Cleaning:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air, Earth, Fire, Water vs DR 5
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air, Earth, Fire, Water Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
