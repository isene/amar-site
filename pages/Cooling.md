Keeps the caster cool (feels like it is up to 10°C cooler per level).

## Casting

To cast Cooling:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air, Earth, Fire, Life, Protection, Water vs DR 6
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air, Earth, Fire, Life, Protection, Water Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
