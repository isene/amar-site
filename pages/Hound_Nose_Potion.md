The drinker's nose wakes: track by scent as a hound does (+5 Tracking, scent-based), know who passed and how long ago. Strong smells during the hour can be briefly incapacitating.

### Ingredients

- A hair of a proven tracking hound.
- Wild garlic (10 g).
- Rain-wet earth (20 g).
- Vinegar (1 dl).
- Shaken, never stirred.
