Creates an impenetrable and immovable wall 1 cm thick. The wall may not be less than 10 cm in any direction. It is almost invisible. If you concentrate you can see a slight shimmering.

## Casting

To cast Wall of Force:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air, Magic vs DR 13
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air, Magic Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
