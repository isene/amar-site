Creates an area of darkness which completely blocks sight. The spell is not cast on any object, and the caster can move it at the rate of 1 m/s per level.

## Casting

To cast Darkness:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Black, Earth vs DR 11
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Black, Earth Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
