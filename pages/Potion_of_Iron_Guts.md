For one day the drinker's stomach fears nothing: poison, spoiled food and foul water do no harm (natural poisons are neutralised; magickal poisons get +6 on the resistance roll). Taste, unfortunately, still works.

### Ingredients

- Charcoal, powdered (30 g).
- Goat's milk (2 dl).
- Nettle, boiled soft (20 g).
- A rusty nail, boiled along and removed.\*
