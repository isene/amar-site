The Ice Shards does d-3 damage per shard. The spell fires 1 shard per level.

The skill of [Pointing](Combat.html#Missile_Weapons) is used to determine if this spell hits its intended target.

## Casting

To cast Ice Shards:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Ice vs DR 7
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Ice Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
