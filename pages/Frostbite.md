Frostbite is a single target spell, which can be blocked. The spell does d-3 + 1 per level damage per round, for a total of 3 rounds.

## Casting

To cast Frostbite:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Ice vs DR 9
- **Cost**: 2 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Ice Attunement 2 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
