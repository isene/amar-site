The drinker falls like a leaf: any fall during the duration lands soft as stepping off a stair. Jumping from great heights on purpose still takes a moment of conviction.

### Ingredients

- Down of a gosling (a pinch).
- A dandelion clock, blown into the vial.
- Spider silk (a strand).
- Rainwater that never touched the ground (1 dl).
