### Physical description

Trollkins are lesser [Trolls](Troll.html). They are distant relatives, but have grown to form their own lesser societies, usually living in forests away from pesky humans. Unfortunately, that is also where the all too dominant [Trolls](Troll.html) live.

### Clothing

Trollkins wear fur or clothes stolen from humans.

### Social structure

Trollkins have strong family bonds. Hierarchy is quite strict. They never betray family members as they do just about everyone else.

### Occupations

When they are not slaving for [Trolls](Troll.html), they are hunters and gatherers.

### Special skills

Trollkins have +3 in Magick Defense.

### Mental description

Trollkins are survivors and adapts easily to new situations (like being bully-slaves for [Trolls](Troll.html). They pretend to be meek while at the same time always looking to gain the upper hand. They backstab at the flip of a coin.
