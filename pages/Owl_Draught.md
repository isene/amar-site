The drinker needs no sleep that night and suffers no fatigue for the missed rest. The next night's sleep is deep, long and non-negotiable.

### Ingredients

- Owl pellet, powdered (5 g).
- Strong black tea leaves (20 g).
- Dew collected before dawn (1 dl).
- A candle burnt at both ends, the wax.
