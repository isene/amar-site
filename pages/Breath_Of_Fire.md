The target the spell has been used on will breathe fire. Anyone within the fire takes d-3 + 1 per level in damage each round.

## Casting

To cast Breath Of Fire:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Fire vs DR 11
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Fire Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
