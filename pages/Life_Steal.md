Steales 1BP per level from a creature and gives it back to the caster. At level 5 the caster can choose what creature that will receive the bodypoints.

## Casting

To cast Life Steal:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Black, Kraagh, Mestronorpha vs DR 8
- **Cost**: 2 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Black, Kraagh, Mestronorpha Attunement 2 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
