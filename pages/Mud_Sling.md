Creates a sling of stiff mud, the sling will be as long as the area of effect of the spell. When the spell is used, the caster chooses where the sling starts. After the duration of the spell, the sling will become normal mud and fall down.

## Casting

To cast Mud Sling:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Swamp vs DR 5
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Swamp Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
