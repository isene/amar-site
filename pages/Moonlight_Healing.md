**The Lunar spells require the moon to be visible.**

When the spell is used, moonlight will shine down on the area of the effect. Everybody inside the moonlight will be healed 1 BP + 1 every level. Healing a broken bone requires a level 3 moonlight healing. To reattach a severed body part you must apply a level 5 moonlight healing within 2 hours.

## Casting

To cast Moonlight Healing:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Lunar vs DR 9
- **Cost**: 2 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 2 rounds
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Lunar Attunement 2 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
