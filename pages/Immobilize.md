Immobilizes the target in the position it was in when the spell took effect. The target will fall unless it has a well balanced stance.

## Casting

To cast Immobilize:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Black, Earth, Life vs DR 13
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Black, Earth, Life Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
