Turns water and dirt into a mudhole trap, that slowly swallows any creature or objects. The mudhole looks like normal mud, but for the duration of the spell the mudhole has a hypnotic ability that pulls creatures towards it.

To recover from the trap requires a Climb roll with DR 5 + 1 per spell level. It takes 1 round per spell level to climb out.

## Casting

To cast Mudhole:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Swamp vs DR 7
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 5 rounds
- **Cooldown**: 7 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Swamp Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
