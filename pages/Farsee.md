Everything looks like it is only one tenth of the actual distance away per level, i.e. one tenth at level 1, one twenthieth at level 2, one thirtieth at level 3, etc. May be turned on and off at will by the target.

## Casting

To cast Farsee:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Life, Perception vs DR 9
- **Cost**: 2 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Life, Perception Attunement 2 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
