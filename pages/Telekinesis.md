Enables the caster to move the object or creature through air with a speed of 1 m/s per level or through water with a speed of ½ m/s per level.

## Casting

To cast Telekinesis:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air vs DR 13
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
