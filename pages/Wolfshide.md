Wrapped in a wolf's hide under the open sky, the caster takes wolf shape until dawn: a wolf's body and senses, the caster's own mind. Clothes and gear do not follow. If the hide is stolen while the ritual runs, so is the way back.

### Ingredients

- A whole wolf's hide, honestly won.\*
- Raw meat, eaten cold.
- Juniper smoke breathed deeply.
- A howl, given without embarrassment.
