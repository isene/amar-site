Detects all disembodied spirits within the area of effect.

## Casting

To cast Detect Disembodied Spirit:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Life, Perception vs DR 8
- **Cost**: 2 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Life, Perception Attunement 2 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
