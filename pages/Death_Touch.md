Does d-1 +2 per level in damage to the creature touched (you need to touch the skin of the creature).

## Casting

To cast Death Touch:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Black vs DR 11
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Black Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
