Enables the target to breathe in air as it was water. It only works on water breathing creatures

## Casting

To cast Air Breathing:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air, Water vs DR 11
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air, Water Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
