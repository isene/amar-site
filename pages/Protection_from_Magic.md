Increases the magic defense of the target by 2 per level.

## Casting

To cast Protection from Magic:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Magic, Protection vs DR 11
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 min.
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Magic, Protection Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
