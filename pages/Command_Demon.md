Allows you to give the demon a command consisting of up to 5 words per level. The demon must understand the command or the spell does not work. The magic defense is doubled if a command goes against the moral code of the target, and tripled if it is self destructive.

## Casting

To cast Command Demon:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Black, Summoning vs DR 11
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Black, Summoning Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
