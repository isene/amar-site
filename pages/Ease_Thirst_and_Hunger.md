You will not feel thirst or hunger while the spell lasts. It does not feed your body, so you will eventually grow weaker and finally die if you go too long without eating or drinking.

## Casting

To cast Ease Thirst and Hunger:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Life vs DR 6
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Life Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
