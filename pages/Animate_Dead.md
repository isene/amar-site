Animates one corpse to a zombie or skeleton (depending on the state of the dead body. The zombie gets 6 BP + 1 BP per 2 levels and melee skill of 1 per level. A skeleton gets 1 BP per 2 levels and 1 per 2 levels in melee skill.

## Casting

To cast Animate Dead:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Black vs DR 13
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 day
- **Cooldown**: 6 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Black Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
