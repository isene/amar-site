Enables you to project thoughts to the target. At level 1 you can project words, at level 3 pictures and at level 5 concepts.

## Casting

To cast Project Thought:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Life vs DR 13
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Life Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
