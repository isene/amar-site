Can move air. You can move the area of effect 1/2 m/s per level. Within the area of effect you can move the air 5 m/s per level. Every 5 m/s of wind gives -1 in melee combat and -2 on missiles.

## Casting

To cast Control Wind:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air vs DR 11
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 min.
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
