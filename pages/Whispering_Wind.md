Creates a weak wind with a message from the caster that will travel to the selected target or targets. The wind moves 5m/s and will die out after telling its message to the targeted creature or creatures.

## Casting

To cast Whispering Wind:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air vs DR 7
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
