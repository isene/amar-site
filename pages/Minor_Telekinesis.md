Enables the caster to move the object or creature through air with a speed of 2 m/s per level or through water with a speed of 1 m/s per level.

## Casting

To cast Minor Telekinesis:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air vs DR 9
- **Cost**: 2 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air Attunement 2 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
