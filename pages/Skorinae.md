Skorinae is a small rocky island off the southeast coast, home to a dozen fisher families.

A half-ruined round watchtower crowns the island's height, and fisher huts cluster by the sheltered landing on the north side. A wrecked sailing ship lies broken on the reef just west.

The tower is older than the kingdom, and the fishers will not climb it after dark.
