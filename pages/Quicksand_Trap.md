Turns dirt and sand into quicksand. The spell can only be used in open areas, and does not effect mountains or buildings.

Detecting the trap requires a Detect Traps roll with DR 3 + 1 per spell level.

To recover from the trap requires a Climb roll with DR 3 +1 per spell level. It takes 1 round per spell level to climb out.

## Casting

To cast Quicksand Trap:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Earth vs DR 9
- **Cost**: 2 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 min
- **Cooldown**: 7 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Earth Attunement 2 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
