Shoots a small tornado towards the target. The tornado will suck up the target, whirling itin a circle for the duration of the spell. For the duration of the spell, the tornado does 1BP + 1 every other level in damage. The target will get -3 in combat while whirling around.

While the air version of the tornado looks normal, the water version of the tornado spell is filled with water instead of air.

## Casting

To cast Tornado:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air, Water vs DR 11
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air, Water Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
