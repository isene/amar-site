After having drunk this potion, the person is able to breathe underwater for 1 hour, but tires easily from physical exertion.

### Ingredients

- Skin from a merman’s tail (100 g).
- A snow lily.
- Running water (1 l).
- Sea salt (50 g).
- Angelica (50 g).
- A poem.
