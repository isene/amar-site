With this spell it is only possible to teleport to a pentagram prepared by the caster. This does not have to be in line of sight, but it must be on top of a solid surface displacing only air.

## Casting

To cast Prepared Teleport:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air vs DR 17
- **Cost**: 6 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 rounds
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air Attunement 5 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 months ritual to transfer spell ownership
