Wall of Ice is an area of effect spell which creates a wall of ice. The spell has 1 m² area per level.

## Casting

To cast Wall of Ice:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Ice vs DR 11
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 2 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Ice Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
