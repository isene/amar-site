Éltasohr is a hamlet of some 150 people (about 30 houses) on the south coast road of Feronir.

The sea lies south, the forest north. Boats rest on the beach; the houses are modest and kept in strict order.

Even here, at the district's edge, the Baron's judgements are posted on the moot post and obeyed.
