A weapon laid in the first light of dawn and sung over burns with Ikalio's favour for the wielder's next fight: +1 Offensive, and the weapon counts as magical against creatures immune to common steel. The favour fades at the fight's end, or at sunset, whichever comes first.

### Ingredients

- First sunlight, caught on the bare blade.
- Oil pressed from sunflower seeds (1 dl).
- A red ribbon tied at the hilt.
- A hymn to the dawn, sung alone.
