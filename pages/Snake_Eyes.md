This ritual will hypnotize the victim so as to obey the caster. He will not however undertake actions directly opposing his survival or basic principles. The caster must have the attention of the victim for the whole casting time of one round or the ritual will not take effect.

### Ingredients

- A tin rod with a 20 carat amethyst.\*
- Myrtle berries (10 g).
- Chicory leaves (10 g).
- Celadine flowers (10 g).
- Snake eyes (2).
