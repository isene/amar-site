Keeps normal sized bugs away from you and anything you are wearing or carrying.

## Casting

To cast Bug Shield:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air, Fire, Life, Magic, Protection vs DR 8
- **Cost**: 2 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air, Fire, Life, Magic, Protection Attunement 2 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
