Tol Paras is a hamlet of some 150 people (about 30 houses) on the coast southeast of Alesir, behind a scatter of bare skerries.

The sea lies east, studded with rocks; forest stands west and north. The road from the northwest ends here, at the last houses before open water.

The skerries break the worst of the storms, and wrecks on them have furnished more than one house in Tol Paras.
