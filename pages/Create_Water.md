Creates fresh water.

## Casting

To cast Create Water:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Water vs DR 9
- **Cost**: 2 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Water Attunement 2 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
