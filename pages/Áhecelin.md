Áhecelin is a village of some 250 people (about 50 houses) in the deep forest south of Lake Lián, built where the north-south road crosses the river on a stone bridge.

The river flows west to east through the village under the old bridge. Forest closes in on all sides, broken by small clearings with fields.

The bridge toll is modest, but everyone traveling between Rauinor and the southern forest pays it.
