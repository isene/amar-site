The drinker turns grey, faint and half-here: mundane weapons pass through (half damage), and once during the duration the drinker may walk through a single wall no thicker than an arm's length. Magick bites as usual. Ghostberries are found where the dead were left unburied.

### Ingredients

- Ghostberries (30 g).
- Grave mist, bottled at dusk.
- Wine gone thin and pale (2 dl).
- A sigh, added last.
