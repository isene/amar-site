Lowers the temperature of the object by 5°C per level. The object heats up normally after the spell ends. It may not be cast on a living creature.

## Casting

To cast Freeze Solid:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Earth vs DR 9
- **Cost**: 2 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Earth Attunement 2 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
