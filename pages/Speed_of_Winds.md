Makes a creature's movement speed faster. Each level the creature's speed will be increased by 50%. When the spell is active, it is possible to see wind behind the creature.

## Casting

To cast Speed of Winds:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air vs DR 9
- **Cost**: 2 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air Attunement 2 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
