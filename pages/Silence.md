Renders the object or creature unable to make any noise. This includes any objects carried or worn by the creature as long as they are worn or carried.

## Casting

To cast Silence:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air vs DR 11
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
