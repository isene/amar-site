This curse will bring bad luck to its target. This is simulated by adjusting all dice rolls concerning the target by one so as to be unfavorable to the target.

### Ingredients

- A part of the target’s body (see Blind Curse).
- The true name of the target.
- Lotus (50 g) and saffron (50 g) burned in an iron kettle.
- A mirror to break.
- A wooden doll made similar to the victim.
- A poem.
- A coordination roll of 8.
