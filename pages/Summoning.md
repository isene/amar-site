Summons a demon with a total of up to 10 per level in its attributes including size. The caster has no control over what kind of demon is summoned.

## Casting

To cast Summoning:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Black, Summoning vs DR 11
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 5 min.
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Black, Summoning Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
