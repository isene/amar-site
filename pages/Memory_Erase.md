Erases a creature or a person's memory.

This spell was used as a punishment on prisoners but a magician used the spell on an innocent person in a trial and the spell was banished by the judge.

## Casting

To cast Memory Erase:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Forbidden Spells vs DR 9
- **Cost**: 2 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Forbidden Spells Attunement 2 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
