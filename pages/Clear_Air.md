Clears the air within the area of effect of any smoke, dust, fog, smell, etc. and keeps it clear for the duration of the spell.

## Casting

To cast Clear Air:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air vs DR 5
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
