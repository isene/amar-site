Ignites flammable materials, see the table below.

| Level | Material               |
|-------|------------------------|
| 1     | Paper, kindling        |
| 2     | Dry porous wood, linen |
| 3     | Dry wood               |
| 4     | Hard, dry wood         |
| 5     | Trees                  |
| 6     | Wet wood               |
| 7     | Dead body              |

## Casting

To cast Ignite:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Fire vs DR 7
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Fire Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
