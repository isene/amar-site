Freeze is a single target, status effect spell which can be blocked. The spell give the target a -1 per level status effect which effects all actions.

## Casting

To cast Freeze:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Ice vs DR 9
- **Cost**: 2 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Ice Attunement 2 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
