Creates a magic cage made out of the magick element of the spell. Nothing gets out of or into the cage while the spell is active. A dispel magic spell of the same element is required to break the cage.

  
The air cage is almost invisible. The fire cage gives warmth but doesn't do damage.

## Casting

To cast Cage O' Magic:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air, Black, Earth, Fire, Water vs DR 9
- **Cost**: 2 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air, Black, Earth, Fire, Water Attunement 2 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
