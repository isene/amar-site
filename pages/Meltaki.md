Meltaki is a village of some 300 people (about 60 houses) on the coast road just east of Calaron.

The sea lies south with a sandy shore; a wooded headland rises east and the open plain spreads north. A jetty and fishing boats work the bay.

The shady tavern by the shore is well known to Calaron's less official traders.
