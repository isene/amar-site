---
title: Protection from Water(EVM)
stats:
  Path(s): Water Path
  A/P?: P
  Resist?: Y
  Casting Time: 1 round
  DR: 4 + the spell's path rank + the spell's level
  Duration: 2 min.
  Range: Touch
  Weight: 75 kg
  Area of Effect: 1O* or 1C*

Protects the object or creature from water and keeps it from getting wet. If it is cast on a creature it also protects any objects it is wearing or carrying.
