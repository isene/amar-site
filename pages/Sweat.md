The targeted creature will become sweaty and get -1, -1 every other level in stauts.

## Casting

To cast Sweat:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Heat vs DR 9
- **Cost**: 2 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Heat Attunement 2 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
