**The Lunar spells require the moon to be visible.**

When the spell is used all animals inside the area of effect that are awake, will protect the caster from other creatures being agressive towards the caster.

## Casting

To cast Lunar Howl:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Lunar vs DR 5
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 3 rounds
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Lunar Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
