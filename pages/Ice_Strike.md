The Ice Strike does d-2 + 1 per level in damage.

The skill of [Pointing](Combat.html#Missile_Weapons) is used to determine if this spell hits its intended target.

## Casting

To cast Ice Strike:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Ice vs DR 9
- **Cost**: 2 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Ice Attunement 2 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
