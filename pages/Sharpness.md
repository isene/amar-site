The weapon does +1 in damage per full 2 levels. It only works on sharp weapons. The weapon is considered magic.

## Casting

To cast Sharpness:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Magic vs DR 11
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 6 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Magic Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
