### Physical description

Giant spiders are not common in Amar. They usually dwell in caves or in forests - depending on their physical appearance (they live where they benefit the most from camouflage. Their bite is potentially lethal, delivering a poison that yields O6 damage after 5 rounds.

### Mental description

Giant spiders a solitaire creatures and partner up only to mate. They are grumpy, cunning and playful only when they kill.
