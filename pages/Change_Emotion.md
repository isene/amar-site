## Casting

To cast Change Emotion:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Life, Perception, Black vs DR 7
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude

- **Cooldown**: 4 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Life, Perception, Black Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
