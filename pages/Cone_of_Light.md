The palm of the caster emits a cone of light.

## Casting

To cast Cone of Light:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Fire, Perception vs DR 9
- **Cost**: 2 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Fire, Perception Attunement 2 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
