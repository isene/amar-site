Leshaethi is a village of some 200 people (about 40 houses) on the coast road southeast of Merisi.

The sea lies southwest; the road runs northwest to southeast through the village with the open plain northeast. A jetty and fishing boats work the shore, and a garrison blockhouse anchors the village, as befits this militarised district.

Alac Geoffryn's riders pass through daily; Leshaethi feeds them, quarters them, and keeps its eyes on the sea.
