title: Balance
categories:
- Physical Skills
- Skills
---

One Balance roll is made for every 5 meters of balancing. If the roll is not successful, the character is stuck and must roll again with a DR of 3 lower to stay at the same place. Another attempt at moving forward may be made, but if this second roll fails, the character falls down. If the initial roll fails by more than 5, the character falls down. Difficulty ratings and adjustments are:

| DR  | Action                                             |
|-----|----------------------------------------------------|
| 0   | Solid beam, 30 cm wide                             |
| 3   | Solid beam, 10 cm wide                             |
| 6   | Solid beam, 5 cm wide                              |
| 9   | Solid beam, 2 cm wide or tight, thick rope         |
| 12  | Solid edge or tight, thin rope or slack thick rope |
| 15  | Slack thin rope                                    |
