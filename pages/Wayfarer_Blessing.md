Those blessed walk the day without tiring: no fatigue from ordinary travel, and their feet find the road even in the dark. The blessing ends the moment a blessed traveller draws a weapon in anger.

### Ingredients

- Dust from a crossroads.
- A worn-out shoe sole, burnt.
- Rosemary (10 g).
- A verse recited while walking backwards.
