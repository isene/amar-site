This curse will decrease the current of one of the target’s attributes (chosen by the caster) by one (to a minimum of one). It can only be performed at midnight under a full moon.

### Ingredients

- A part of the target’s body (see Blind Curse).
- The true name of the target.
- Lead and mercury boiled in a stone bowl.
- A long poem.
- A coordination roll of 6.
