Can move fire along the ground, up walls, etc., but not across water. You can move the area of effect 1/2 m/s per level. Within the area of effect you can move the fire 1 m/s per level.

## Casting

To cast Control Fire:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Fire vs DR 13
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 min.
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Fire Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
