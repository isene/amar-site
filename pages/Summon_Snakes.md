Summons one snake per level. Snakes has 3BP + 1 per level and an unarmed skill of 3 + 1 per level.

| Level | Description |
|----|----|
| Level 1 | The snake only takes combat orders. |
| Level 2 | The snakes takes all types of orders. |
| Level 3 | The snakes does poison damage on a successful hit, 2BP each round until poison is outjected. |

## Casting

To cast Summon Snakes:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Swamp vs DR 5
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 3 rounds
- **Cooldown**: 7 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Swamp Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
