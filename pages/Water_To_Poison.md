Turns water into the poison.

**Magick Poison Types**

| Level | Description |
|----|----|
| Level 1 | 1BP in damage. |
| Level 2 | 2BP in damage and -1 in status until the poison is removed. |
| Level 3 | 3BP in damage and -2 in status until the poison is removed. |
| Level 4 | The poison temporarily disables the location the poison has been injected into. As long as the poison is in the bodypart, the location will be disabled. If the poison is consumed, roll an hit location roll to see what body is affected. |
| Level 5 | The creature will instantly vomit, if the creature is in combat they will lose the ability to fight and defend. |

## Casting

To cast Water To Poison:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Poison vs DR 7
- **Cost**: 1 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 2 rounds
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Poison Attunement 1 or higher
- **Giving**: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
