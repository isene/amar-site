Can move ice along the ground or in water. You can move the area of effect ½ m/s per level. Within the area of effect you can move the ice 1 m/s per level.

## Casting

To cast Control Ice:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Ice vs DR 13
- **Cost**: 3 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 min.
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Ice Attunement 3 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
