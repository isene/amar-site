This amulet will protect against possession. For this purpose only, it will increase the bearer’s magick resistance by 5.

### Ingredients

- A fennel flower.
- A dog’s heart.
- The brain of a 1 day old body (same race as the bearer).
- A writing roll of 8 (due to complex inscriptions).
