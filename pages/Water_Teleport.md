Put the object or creature in a bubble and teleports it. It is only possible to teleport in water.

## Casting

To cast Water Teleport:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Water vs DR 15
- **Cost**: 4 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Water Attunement 5 or higher
- **Giving**: 4 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
