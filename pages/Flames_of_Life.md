This ritual must be performed with a fire at least the size of a torch no more than 1 meter away from the caster. The ritual causes flames to shoot forth from the fire and hit the intended target in the chest, causing d in damage. The flames cannot be dodged as they will be magically guided towards the heart of the victim.

### Ingredients

- A 10 carat ruby.\*
- A fire lily picked on midsummer day.
- A small poem including a description of the target.
