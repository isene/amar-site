Recolar is perhaps the most noteworthy among the lesser Gods. He is worshipped by sports contenders and those engaging in physical contests out of fun rather than dire need - thus gladiators would worship Taroc rather than Recolar. Recolar is the son of Taroc and a Demigod Queen, Lahera Aliasini.
