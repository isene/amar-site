It is only possible to teleport on top of a solid surface displacing air.

## Casting

To cast Teleport:

- **Roll**: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air vs DR 17
- **Cost**: 6 Mental Fortitude
- **Time**: 1 round
- **Cooldown**: 3 hours

## Transfer Requirements

- **Receiving**: Requires Air Attunement 5 or higher
- **Giving**: 2 months ritual to transfer spell ownership
