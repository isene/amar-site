    To cast Divine Rune Of Magic:

    • Roll: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Earth vs DR 13
    • Cost: 3 Mental Fortitude
    • Time: 1 min.
    • Cooldown: 3 hours

    Transfer Requirements

    • Receiving: Requires Earth Attunement 3 or higher
    • Giving: 2 weeks ritual to transfer spell ownership
