    Magick / Potions

    Bear Potion

    DR
    10
    Duration
    1 year
    Production time
    3 days
    Expiration
    1 year
    Market price
    50 sp

    The strength of a person is doubled after drinking this potion.

    Ingredients

    • Blood from a giant (1 dl).
    • Blood from a cave lion or bear (1/2 dl) from an animal killed under a full moon.
    • Betony (10 g).
    • An iron container.*
    • A poem.

    Listed under Potions.
