    Magick / Rituals

    Rustwither

    Path(s)
    Ritual
    Resist?
    N
    Casting Time
    1 round
    DR
    7
    Duration
    Instant
    Range
    Touch
    Area of Effect
    1 metal object

    Ages one metal object a hundred years in a heartbeat: locks crumble, blades pit and dull (double damage to the weapon's hit points), hinges seize. Dwarven work resists with DR +4.

    Ingredients

    • Red rust scraped from a shipwreck (20 g).
    • Vinegar in which a key has soaked a week (1 dl).
    • A drop of the caster's blood.
    • A word of unmaking, whispered.

    Listed under Rituals.
