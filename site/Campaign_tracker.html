    Campaign tracker

    To help you as the Game Master keep track of events in the campaign, copy the template below and enter what happens in the game into the appropriate dates. Save your file as Year-XXX.txt (with XXX being the current year in the game, starting with 354). Holy days are shown in parenthesis.
