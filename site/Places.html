    The world

    Places

    • Anae Asthir Anae Asthir is a village of some 200 people (about 40 houses) on the east coast of Aleresir, between the old forest and the sea.
    • Arthidor Arthidor is a fishing village of some 150 people, part houses and part tents, on the low wooded headland south of Tzierengit.
    • Bish Nathir Bish Nathir is a bleak hamlet of ten weathered houses at the very tip of the southern cape, the southernmost dwelling in the kingdom.
    • Caer Ilias Caer Ilias is a grey stone castle in a forest clearing, held for the Baron of Rauinir.
    • Canarestá Canarestá is a village of some 250 people (about 50 timber houses) on the northeastern shore of Lake Ámia, beneath the northern mountains.
    • Clifton Clifton is a quarry village of some 350 people (about 70 houses) on the slopes of the hills west of the central plain.
    • Ijantor Ijantor is a lone watchtower island two nautical miles off the Mieronir coast, a couple of hundred metres across.
    • Keron Keron is a timber town of some 350 people (about 70 houses) in a great clearing on the east-west forest road.
    • Laer Alghir Laer Alghir is a village of some 200 people (about 40 houses) on the coast road northwest of Calaron.
    • Leshaethi Leshaethi is a village of some 200 people (about 40 houses) on the coast road southeast of Merisi.
    • Lienthaer Lienthaer is a farming village of some 400 people (about 80 houses) on the south bank of the river east of Amaron.
    • Lientros Lientros is a village of some 300 people (about 60 timber houses) on the southwestern shore of Lake Ámia, where the river leaves the lake southward.
    • Meltaki Meltaki is a village of some 300 people (about 60 houses) on the coast road just east of Calaron.
    • Molthaki Molthaki is a village of some 250 people (about 50 houses) in a forest clearing west of Keron, on the same road.
    • Orethali Orethali is a village of some 200 people (about 40 houses) where the forest road meets the eastern sea.
    • Phenaihír Phenaihír is a village of some 250 people (about 50 houses) on Feronir's south coast road.
    • Saethor Saethor is a village of some 200 people (about 40 houses) on the east side of the southern cape, on the road toward Bish Nathir.
    • Skorinae Skorinae is a small rocky island off the southeast coast, home to a dozen fisher families.
    • Soraeq Soraeq is a hamlet of some 100 people (about 20 houses) gathered around a great timber longhouse at the western outlet of Lake Lián.
    • Tiárn Tiárn is a stone tower keep at the western end of the mountain lake northwest of Amaron, guarding the northern pass.
    • Tol Paras Tol Paras is a hamlet of some 150 people (about 30 houses) on the coast southeast of Alesir, behind a scatter of bare skerries.
    • Tsenkir Tsenkir is the largest village in Amar (population 500, about 100 houses) and the kingdom's most important place of trading.
    • Tzierengit Tzierengit is a road-junction village of some 400 people (about 80 houses) at a small natural harbour on the south coast.
    • Áhecelin Áhecelin is a village of some 250 people (about 50 houses) in the deep forest south of Lake Lián, built where the north-south road crosses the river on a stone bridge.
    • Élein Élein is a fortified hamlet of some 150 people (about 30 houses) on the east bank of the border river, facing the Araxi woods.
    • Élsor Élsor is a fortified village of some 200 people (about 40 houses) at the timber bridge over the border river, south of Élein.
    • Éltasohr Éltasohr is a hamlet of some 150 people (about 30 houses) on the south coast road of Feronir.
