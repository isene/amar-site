    Magick / Rituals

    Catch Fire

    Path(s)
    Ritual
    Resist?
    N
    Casting Time
    1 round
    DR
    6
    Duration
    Instant
    Range
    10 m
    Area of Effect
    1 object

    This ritual will create fire. To make the fire, the caster must have a flammable object with a maximum volume of 30 cm³ to start burning as the effect of this ritual.

    Ingredients

    • Ash from a torch.
    • A 3 carat ruby.*
    • A fire lily.
    • Powdered salamander blood (10 g). Blood from a dragon reduces the difficulty rating by 3.
    • A poem.
    • A coordination roll of 8.

    Listed under Rituals.
