    Add-ons / Evolutionary Magick

    Water Path

    These are the Water Path spells of the Amar Evolutionary Magick System.

    Spell Description Requirements
    Rank 1
    Cleaning Cleans the area, comparable to sweeping, dusting, mopping. Does not get rid of really ingrained dirt. Any potensial dirt that is created during the duration will be cleaned. Water Sphere
    Rain Shield Forms an invisible umbrella over the caster which repels rain.
    Rank 2
    Create Water Creates fresh water. Cleaning
    Polish Cleans the object(s) thoroughly leaving them gleaming as if polished. Cleaning
    Rank 3
    Dry Dries out the object(s) and/or creature(s) targeted. Note that when used on living creatures (including plants) it only dries the surface, not the body itself. Polish
    Protection from Water Protects the object or creature from water and keeps it from getting wet. If it is cast on a creature it also protects any objects it is wearing or carrying.

    Rain Shield
    Rank 4
    Fog Creates fog. Sight within the fog is limited to 2 meters, and combat modifiers for moonlight applies. Create Water
    Zap Zaps a target. If the target is in contact with water it leaves them with -1 in combat status.
    Rank 5
    Water of Restoration Creates fresh water capable of healing creatures. Restores 1BP to a creature for each liter it drinks. Create Water, Lesser Healing
    Rank 6
    Water Barrier Creates a circular barrier of water that will slowdown projectiles. Create Water, Protection from Water
