    Amar

    A realistic role-playing game you learn in an evening, played with one six-sided die.

    Skill + O6 ≥ DR

    Every roll works the same way. Add your skill to an open-ended die roll (O6) and meet or beat the Difficulty Rating (DR).

    Roll a 6 and you roll again, adding 1 for every 4 or higher. Roll a 1 and you roll again, taking away 1 for every 3 or lower.

    About Amar

    Amar is two things: a realistic rule system, and the fantasy world where the game takes place.

    The game is the result of more than 35 years of development and play testing. Its forerunner, The Mega Role-Playing System, was published in the UK in 1987.

    Mega aimed to be the most realistic RPG, and so it was complicated. Geir Isene and Bent Brakas cut the rules down again and again while keeping the realism.

    The Game Master learns the game in an evening. The players catch on in an hour or two.
