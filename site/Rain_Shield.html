    Rain Shield

    Domain
    Air, Magic, Protection, Water
    Encumbrance
    2
    Cooldown
    3 hours
    Casting Time
    1 round
    Active/Passive
    Active
    Restrictions
    None
    DR
    6
    Cost
    1 Mental Fortitude
    Distance
    0m
    Duration
    10 min.
    Area of Effect
    ½ m radius
    Receiving
    Air, Magic, Protection, Water Attunement 1
    Giving
    Ritual 1 week

    Forms an invisible umbrella over the caster which repels rain.

    Casting

    To cast Rain Shield:

    • Roll: O6 + SPIRIT + Attunement + Air, Magic, Protection, Water vs DR 6
    • Cost: 1 Mental Fortitude
    • Time: 1 round
    • Cooldown: 3 hours

    Transfer Requirements

    • Receiving: Requires Air, Magic, Protection, Water Attunement 1 or higher
    • Giving: 1 week ritual to transfer spell ownership
