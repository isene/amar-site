    People and creatures

    NPCs

    Here you will find some of the most interesting NPCs (Non-Player Characters) in Amar.

    Some of the characters are created by the Console Version (text output) of the NPC generator in Amar Tools.

    • Alac Geoffryn Taroc warlord ruling Mieronir; relative of the King, trusted with the defence of the kingdom.
    • Altira Altira is Amar's greatest huntress.
    • Archangelos Archangelos is the High Lord of Amar.
    • Ayah Respected adventurer, travelling with Ran-Asar and the puma Mira.
    • Baron Fer Chalun Baron of Rauinir; a Walmaer Lord and eager fisherman, said to commune with the fish and command the waters.
    • Baron Garos Maella Baron of Feronir; ex-Moltan initiate, now self-proclaimed ruler, judge, jury and executor.
    • Baronesse Fienna Milin Baroness of Calaronir, the King's cousin.
    • Boran Fighter of the Wayanah-party; a mountain that walks.
    • Commander Seillan Torthal Commander of the kingdom's army; a Taroc warlord, scarred and unbending.
    • Elysian Chakorta Elysian Chakorta is Amar's Cal Amae Overlord.
    • Eris Priestess of The Guards; wife of Kator.
    • Ferro One of the three playful wizards (Fire).
    • GarThaleon The Master Thief of Amar, GarThaleon is the "king" of the underworld.
    • High Priest Larosmarot High Priest of the biggest Walmaer temple; very clean and proper.
    • Hina Warrior of the Wayanah-party; the shield-wall made person.
    • Kator Knight; leader of The Guards, a mercenary party.
    • King Caolain II King of Amar; a Walmaer worshipper.
    • King Gorm King of the dwarves, ruling from Borgheim in the Dvergfjellene.
    • Kiro Notorious Perception wizard; one of the very few permitted to use magick inside Calaron's walls.
    • Lady Serena Chiall Principal of Aleresir; a young, single Anashina priestess with a strong belief in personal freedom.
    • Lagioracharan Scout of the Wayanah-party; the first in and the last seen.
    • Lor Aigilos Lor Aigilos is perhaps the greatest warrior in Amar.
    • Lord Nalchir Emaron Lord of Land in Tsenkir, the largest village in Amar and the kingdom's most important place of trading.
    • Lord Thainir Laidhos The King's cousin, looked upon as the Mayor of Amaron; directly responsible for law and order in the capital.
    • Manir One of the three playful wizards (Life).
    • Mizamir Archeress of the Wayanah-party; threads a bowstring through a keyhole at two hundred paces.
    • Moltar the Just An offbeat Moltan priest; right hand of Baron Garos Maella.
    • Naraghin Wizard with a magick shop; trades spells.
    • Orach Warrior of The Guards; the company's anvil.
    • Povi One of the three playful wizards (Air).
    • Prince Vaiangor The prince; a sports enthusiast and idol figure for healthy living.
    • Princess Ianira The princess; singer, painter, poet and flautist.
    • Queen Fiona Queen of Amar; a Shalissa priestess.
    • Ran-Asar Respected adventurer, travelling with Ayah and the puma Mira; likely pursuing mysterious missions.
    • Raven Demon Xetus, the Raven Demon, is a summoned trader of forbidden spells.
    • Salidus Taramostran Salidus Taramostran is rumored to be the greatest living wizard in Amar.
    • The Rai Leader of the Magick Circle, the eight most powerful wizards of Amar; a summoning specialist.
    • Vinar Ranger of The Guards; reads the land like a ledger.
    • Wayanah Warrior-sorceress; leader of the renowned Wayanah-party of high-level adventurers.
    • Yaloosi Wizard of The Guards; a Life and Protection wizard, full of humour and joy, loves all kinds of games.
    • Zarin Travelling alchemist; merchant in useful potions with fair pricing.
